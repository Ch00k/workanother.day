The register

{% explainer "register-help" %}

The ewidencja przychodów art. 15 requires to be kept, rather than a report about one. From 1 January 2027 the law requires it to be kept in software able to produce the JPK_EWP XML, which is what this is.

Entries come from two places. Every issued invoice becomes one, dated by its revenue date - art. 14 ust. 1e makes an issued invoice revenue whether or not it has been paid, while a draft is a document nobody holds. Every exchange difference becomes another, dated the day the money landed and carrying the rate of the invoice it arose on, a negative difference as a negative amount.

A correction invoice is an invoice for this purpose, entered for the difference it made and dated by the corrected invoice's revenue date, with a note naming what it restates.
