1. Getting in

Log in to eZUS and open the business account, the one for the payer. ePłatnik is at the bottom of its left menu, and opens in eZUS's older green look. There: Kreatory, then Obsługa rozliczenia.

ePłatnik switches itself on the first time it is opened. A notice about correction deadlines may come up on the way in; OK closes it.

8. Wyślij i zakończ

Signed with Podpis ePUAP, which is Profil Zaufany, in a window pz.gov.pl opens, so it has to be let open pop-ups. A login through login.gov.pl is not asked for again. It ends on Dokumenty zostały wysłane.

9. Afterwards

  1. The proof is in ePłatnik under Dokumenty wysłane: the DRA, then Podgląd poświadczenia przedłożenia.

co najmniej jednej formy opodatkowania No tax form ticked, or a revenue or income field filled that is not the ryczałt one.
wszystkie i tylko wymagane podstawy A base given for an insurance not held, such as chorobowe that was not elected.
