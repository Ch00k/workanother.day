1. Getting in

Log in to eZUS and open the business account, the one for the payer. ePłatnik is at the bottom of its left menu, and opens in eZUS's older green look. There: Kreatory, then Obsługa rozliczenia.

ePłatnik switches itself on the first time it is opened. A notice about correction deadlines may come up on the way in; OK closes it.

2. Witamy w kreatorze

The payer's details, read only. Check the NIP is {{ seller.nip }}, then Dalej.

3. Wybór trybu

Nowy komplet rozliczeniowy. A first filing and a correction are told apart here and by the identyfikator; there is no box for it.

8. Wyślij i zakończ

Signed with Podpis ePUAP, which is Profil Zaufany, in a window pz.gov.pl opens, so it has to be let open pop-ups. A login through login.gov.pl is not asked for again. It ends on Dokumenty zostały wysłane.

9. Afterwards

  1. The proof is in ePłatnik under Dokumenty wysłane: the DRA, then Podgląd poświadczenia przedłożenia.
  2. {{ month.date|date:"F" }}'s page records it with I have filed it, and states the transfer of {{ month.dra_total|money }}.

When Weryfikuj objects

Payer-only mode refused ZUS holds nobody insured under the payer's PESEL for the month. The ZZA or ZUA is what registers them.
co najmniej jednej formy opodatkowania No tax form ticked, or a revenue or income field filled that is not the ryczałt one.
wszystkie i tylko wymagane podstawy A base given for an insurance not held, such as chorobowe that was not elected.
Brak zgłoszenia z podanym kodem tytułu ZUS holds the payer under another kod. Ubezpieczenia in eZUS shows which; the registration is what has to change.
Stopa procentowa składki wypadkowej jest niezgodna The rate on Ogólne opcje deklaracji is not the one ZUS holds.
Dokument o podanym lub wyższym identyfikatorze został już przekazany This month's DRA has already gone. A correction to it is numbered 02.
Nie prowadziłeś cały poprzedni rok The previous year's revenue option was ticked.
