{% explainer "revenue-pln-help" %}

PLN is the currency every Polish threshold, rate and return is written in. This sits beside the invoice rather than on it: with no VAT to state, no element of art. 106e asks for a PLN figure, so it is a record of the revenue rather than part of the document.

The revenue date is the last day of the service period under art. 14 ust. 1e, and the figure is the invoice converted at the NBP rate of the last working day before it. The rate, the table it came from and its date are stated with every figure, which is what makes one checkable.

{% if invoice.is_correction %}

A correction is converted at the rate the invoice it corrects was converted at rather than at one of its own: it restates revenue already booked, and at any other rate a korekta unwinding an invoice in full would leave a remainder in zlote behind it.

{% else %}

Art. 6 ust. 1c of the ryczalt act applies art. 24c PIT, so the revenue is also worth whatever it was worth on the day the money landed. The difference between the two days adjusts it, and goes into the register dated the day of payment.
