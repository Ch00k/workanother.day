{% explainer "month-owes-help" %}

Both fall due on the 20th of the month after this one, moved to the next working day where that is a Saturday or a day off. The ryczałt is on this month's revenue less the contributions art. 11 allows against it; the contributions are on a base that follows the regime, and neither of them is what was paid.

Recording the payments keeps these figures as they stand now. A correction that later moves the month leaves the payment where it was, and the two are shown side by side for the disagreement to be seen.
