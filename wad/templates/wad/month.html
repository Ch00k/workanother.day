What the month owes

{% explainer "month-owes-help" %}

Both fall due on the 20th of the month after this one, moved to the next working day where that is a Saturday or a day off. The ryczałt is on this month's revenue less the contributions art. 11 allows against it; the contributions are each a percentage of a base that follows the regime, and neither of them is what was paid.

The base and the ryczałt are each rounded to whole złote under art. 63 § 1 Ordynacji podatkowej, and every contribution to the grosz. What each line was struck from stands beside it, so the whole of both sums can be checked.

Recording the payments keeps these figures as they stand now. A correction that later moves the month leaves the payment where it was, and the two are shown side by side for the disagreement to be seen.
