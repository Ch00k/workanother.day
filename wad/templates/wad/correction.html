{% explainer "correction-help" %}

A korekta restates the invoice it corrects rather than replacing it. Both states go on the document and KSeF takes the difference between them, so a withdrawn line stays on it as the state before rather than disappearing, and withdrawing every line unwinds the invoice.

The reason is printed on the correction and sent with it. Art. 106e has not required one since 2022 and this does: it is the only thing on the document that says what happened.

Art. 14 ust. 1m dates the two kinds of correction apart. One that puts a mistake right restates revenue that arose when the invoice said it did, so it goes back into that month. One caused by something that happened afterwards is revenue of the month the korekta is issued in, which can be a later month and a later year.

It is saved as a draft, dated today and numbered in {{ corrected.number }}'s own month, and issued from its own page the same way an invoice is. Whichever month it lands in, it is converted at the exchange rate the corrected invoice was converted at.
