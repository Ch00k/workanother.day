Health contribution

{% explainer "health-bases-help" %}

Art. 81 ust. 2e of the ustawa o świadczeniach opieki zdrowotnej sets the base at 60, 100 or 180 percent of the average wage, according to revenue accumulated from the start of the year. The percentages and the two revenue thresholds are in the act; the wage they are taken from is announced every January.

The one to take is przeciętne miesięczne wynagrodzenie w sektorze przedsiębiorstw włącznie z wypłatami z zysku, in the fourth quarter of the year before, announced by the Prezes GUS in Monitor Polski under art. 5 pkt 31. GUS issues a second obwieszczenie the same day — bez wypłat nagród z zysku, some 34 grosze away and for something else entirely.

Check the monthly contributions stated back against the ones ZUS publishes. They are 9 percent of each base.

Social contributions

{% explainer "social-bases-help" %}

Art. 18a ust. 1 ustawy o sus charges the preferential period on 30 percent of the minimum wage, set by rozporządzenie Rady Ministrów, and art. 18 ust. 8 charges everyone else on 60 percent of the prognozowane przeciętne wynagrodzenie miesięczne from the budget act.

The minimum wage can step mid-year, and has twice recently: 3 490 to 3 600 in July 2023 and 4 242 to 4 300 in July 2024. The preferential base steps with it, and so does the threshold Fundusz Pracy is owed from, so a year holds both figures and the month decides which is in force. Leave the July field empty where the year holds one wage.

Check the full base against what ZUS publishes and the preferential one against biznes.gov.pl. Wypadkowe is stated at {{ accident_rate|floatformat:"-2" }}%, the rate for a payer reporting at most nine insured; a taxpayer assigned another rate carries it on the taxpayer.
