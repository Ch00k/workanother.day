Health contribution

{% explainer "health-help" %}

The band follows revenue accumulated from the start of the year, less social contributions paid, and a threshold crossed in a month is paid at the higher amount from that month on with no correction of earlier ones at the time.

The annual settlement then recomputes every one of the year's insured months at the band the year ends in and charges the difference, which is the provision stated here. A negative one is a refund, and it has to be claimed rather than arriving.

The bases are 60, 100 and 180 percent of the average wage in the fourth quarter of the year before, announced each January and fetched by nothing here. They are national figures, one set a year for the whole instance, so entering the wage the three are worked out from belongs to whoever runs it.

PIT-28 for {{ year }}

{% explainer "pit-28-figures-help" %}

Art. 11 ust. 1 deducts social contributions paid and art. 11 ust. 1a half the health contribution paid, both on a cash basis, so what a year deducts is what was paid during it — the transfers below, whichever month each settles.

The base and the tax are each rounded to whole złote under art. 63 § 1 Ordynacji podatkowej. This is the year's own figure, taken over the whole of it rather than the twelve monthly ones added up, and the two legitimately disagree: a month whose deductions outrun its revenue cannot carry the excess forward and the year can.

A payment is not a deduction, so nothing above moves when one is recorded. What it changes is the balance the return settles: the year's tax less the ryczałt paid for its months. A negative balance is an overpayment, which the return claims back.

Figures to check against, not a return.
