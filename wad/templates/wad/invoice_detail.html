{% explainer "ksef-help" %}

KSeF is Poland's national e-invoicing system. An invoice sent there is issued by KSeF accepting it: the number it gives back and the link that verifies it are what make the document an invoice, and until then this is a draft nobody holds.

It is authorised with the seller's KSeF token, and both parties are identified by tax identifier rather than by name - which is why a buyer without one falls outside it.

The date on the invoice has to be the day it is sent. KSeF treats an earlier one as issued offline, which needs a certificate and a second QR code this does not produce.

Sending ends with the invoice in flight rather than accepted: KSeF processes it afterwards, so this page waits and says what came back. A rejected invoice keeps the reason in KSeF's own words and can be sent again once the cause is fixed.
